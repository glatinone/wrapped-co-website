# WRAPPED.CO Website Concept — Approval Draft

## Recommended direction: Editorial Light

The website should not be entirely black. A warm-ivory interface makes product imagery and typography easier to read, while dark photography preserves the intimate mood. This gives the brand a richer rhythm than a uniformly dark site and improves shopping clarity.

### Homepage flow

1. **Header** — wordmark, Shop, Our Story, Scents, Contact, Bag.
2. **Hero** — “Scent, wrapped in a moment.” with one collection CTA and a strong product photograph.
3. **Signature scents** — three featured candles with scent family, size, and price visible before hover.
4. **The ritual** — short brand story paired with a close detail of wax, wick, or hand-poured process.
5. **Scent finder** — three simple choices: mood, scent family, and room.
6. **Worcester note** — local identity, pickup/shipping information if applicable.
7. **Email capture** — understated “Notes from WRAPPED.CO,” not an intrusive popup.
8. **Footer** — contact, Instagram, policies, candle-care guide.

### Product page essentials

- Large product image and one lifestyle image
- Scent name, short atmosphere line, notes, burn time, wax/wick details, size, and price
- Add-to-bag button visible without scrolling on desktop
- Candle care in an accordion
- Shipping/pickup clarity for Worcester, MA
- Related scents based on mood rather than a generic carousel

### Copy starter

**Hero:** Scent, wrapped in a moment.

**Support:** Thoughtfully poured candles created to make ordinary rooms feel considered.

**Primary CTA:** Explore the collection

### Two optional variations

- **Dark Gallery:** near-black interface, cinematic photography, stronger launch/editorial feel; more dramatic but less effortless for browsing.
- **Soft Atelier:** warmer paper tones, more process photography, softer typography; friendlier and more handmade, but less formal.

**Recommendation:** approve Editorial Light as the base, then borrow the Dark Gallery treatment for campaign and launch pages.

## Information needed before implementation

- Final Instagram handle, email, and phone number
- Product list, scent names, prices, sizes, burn times, and stock status
- Shipping area and whether local Worcester pickup is offered
- Checkout platform preference (for example Shopify)
- Final product photography or approval to produce a shot list
