# WRAPPED.CO — Brand Direction 01

## Core idea

**Quiet ritual, carefully wrapped.** WRAPPED.CO should feel intimate, modern, and considered: a small luxury that belongs in an everyday room rather than a decorative “luxury” object that feels distant.

The identity uses a folded ribbon that also reads as a flame. It connects the name **WRAPPED.CO** to the candle category without relying on a generic candle illustration.

## Visual system

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Primary mark | Wide-spaced serif wordmark with a small ribbon-flame symbol |
| Carbon | `#141210` — primary packaging and type |
| Warm ivory | `#F3EDE3` — background and reverse type |
| Antique brass | `#B08A5A` — restrained foil/detail accent |
| Smoke | `#68615A` — secondary copy |
| Display type | Cormorant Garamond / Canela-style editorial serif |
| Utility type | Manrope / Neue Haas-style clean sans serif |
| Texture | Matte uncoated paper, blind emboss, black glass, limited foil |

## Usage principles

- Keep layouts spacious; the empty space is part of the identity.
- Use brass only for the flame, a short rule, or one small point of emphasis.
- Avoid ornamental borders, floral illustrations, and multiple decorative motifs.
- Product names may use larger serif typography; functional details stay in a small sans serif.
- Photography should use warm directional light, dark natural materials, and visible texture.

## Suggested voice

- Calm, sensory, concise.
- Describe atmosphere before fragrance notes.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as “the world’s best” or generic “premium quality.”

Sample brand line: **Scent, wrapped in a moment.**

## Initial applications

1. Business card: no personal name; brand on the front, contact details and Worcester, MA on the back.
2. Candle label: ribbon-flame, brand, scent name, product type, and net weight only.
3. Rigid box: blind-embossed black paper with a small antique-brass emblem.
4. Social launch post: product close-up, oversized serif announcement, minimal supporting copy.
5. Website: warm-ivory editorial storefront with dark product photography.

## Production notes

- Business card size: 3.5 × 2 in trim, with 0.125 in bleed on every side.
- Recommended stock: 32 pt black duplex or 600–700 gsm uncoated black stock.
- Recommended finish: blind deboss plus one-color warm ivory or muted gold foil.
- The contact data in the current SVG is sample data and must be confirmed before printing.
