# WRAPPED.CO — Brand concept package

This folder contains a first-round visual direction for review before website implementation.

## Files

- `assets/wrappedco-logo-primary.svg` — editable primary wordmark and ribbon-flame mark
- `assets/business-card-front.svg` — print-sized front, including 0.125 in bleed
- `assets/business-card-back.svg` — print-sized back, including 0.125 in bleed
- `assets/candle-label.svg` — editable front label concept
- `mockups/wrappedco-brand-product-concept.png` — AI-generated packaging/product visualization
- `website-concept/wrappedco-homepage-concept.png` — AI-generated homepage direction
- `brand-direction.md` — concise brand system and production guidance
- `website-concept/concept-notes.md` — proposed structure, copy direction, and approval choices

## Before production

Replace the sample contact details on the business card with the confirmed phone number, Instagram handle, and email address. The SVGs are editable in Figma, Illustrator, Affinity Designer, or Inkscape.
