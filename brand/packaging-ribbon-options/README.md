# WRAPPED.CO packaging directions

All three directions use the approved slim gold ribbon mark and the original restrained black, warm ivory, and muted brass palette.

## 01. Gallery Black

A near-monochrome black vessel and rigid box with a fine gold seam. This is the most minimal and formal direction.

## 02. Ivory Wrap

A matte black vessel and box held by a warm ivory paper wrap. The wrap creates a recognizable unboxing detail while keeping production practical. This is the recommended hero direction.

## 03. Wrapped Column

A cylindrical packaging family with narrow ivory seals. The vertical system gives each scent its own title while keeping the collection unmistakably WRAPPED.CO.

The images are visual development mockups. Final dielines, legal copy, vessel dimensions, fragrance names, and print finishes should be confirmed with the packaging vendor before production.
